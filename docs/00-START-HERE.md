# START HERE — what this repository is

A third-party construction (no affiliation with Sir Roger Penrose) offered to his program.
Penrose's program has **states and reductions** — objective reduction of the wave function,
the non-computability of mathematical understanding, conformal cyclic cosmology — but no
model of the **observer's position**: who stands where when the collapse is delivered.

## The proposal (speculative, tagged [EXTENSION] throughout)

1. **The observer is position zero**: a stationary point, the witness. The origin of a
   coordinate system is a point *in* the space it coordinates — there is no view from
   nowhere.
2. **Collapse is recognition, not force**: consistent with OR's self-collapse reading; a
   forcing of a definite outcome diverges (as conjugate-gradient iteration on an
   indefinite matrix diverges), while recognition performs the selection.
3. **Superposition is the house of the could-have-been**: the unchosen alternatives live in
   the indefinite directions of an indefinite (Krein-type) metric; one direction is
   rendered definite per act.
4. **The non-computability bridge**: "there is no observation from nowhere" is offered as
   the metaphysical form of Penrose's argument that understanding is non-computable.
   Issue #1 asks for Goedel-style rigor: show the observer's selection performs what no
   Turing machine can.
